WHY IT IS SHAPED LIKE THIS
==========================
--c      the article column: min(780px, 100vw - the theme's clamp() side padding)
--narrow the height the stacked phone layout needs, as a line through the measurements
--wide   the two-column layout from a 560px column up. Its height falls to about a
         660px column and rises again after, so this is the higher of two lines
         rather than one, which keeps it within ~20px instead of ~60px
--gate   0 above a 577px column, huge below it, so `min()` picks --narrow on phones
         and --wide on everything else. 577 rather than 560 on purpose: on Windows
         with classic scrollbars 100vw is about 17px wider than the real column, and
         a step at 560 would hand a narrow-layout page the short wide-layout height
height   never shorter than the content at any of the 28 widths measured; 7px over at
         a 780px desktop column, 24px over on average

NO GAP UNDER THE FRAME
======================
Two things do this, both already in place:
- `display:block` stops the iframe sitting on a text baseline, which otherwise leaves
  a few px of descender space beneath it.
- `?fill=1` makes the page stretch to the frame and the card stage take up the slack,
  so any pixels the formula over-estimates become background around the card inside
  the graphic. The graphic's own background always reaches the bottom edge of the
  frame, so there is never a strip of page showing through.
Checked end to end at 375, 414, 626, 700, 860 and 1280px viewports: no vertical or
horizontal scrollbar, and the background meets the frame edge every time.

IF THE STYLE IS STRIPPED
========================
Some setups sanitise `--custom-properties` out of an inline style. The same formula
written out in full, with no custom properties:

[iframe src="https://monga2026098aatimeline.vercel.app/?fill=1" width="100%" scrolling="no" loading="lazy" title="How the invasion escalated" style="display:block; width:100%; border:0; height:min(calc(1237px - 0.64 * min(780px, 100vw - 2*clamp(20px, 1px + 5vw, 40px))), calc(max(calc(1193px - 0.733 * min(780px, 100vw - 2*clamp(20px, 1px + 5vw, 40px))), calc(505px + 0.29 * min(780px, 100vw - 2*clamp(20px, 1px + 5vw, 40px)))) + max(0px, calc((577px - min(780px, 100vw - 2*clamp(20px, 1px + 5vw, 40px))) * 1000))));"][/iframe]

LINKING TO ONE MOMENT
=====================
Add &step=N (1-7) to open on that card, e.g. ...?fill=1&step=4

AFTER ANY COPY CHANGE
=====================
Re-measure and refit: dev/heights.json holds the 28 measured column widths, and the
README says how. The footer source line is the thing that sets the height at the
narrowest columns, so edit it with care.
